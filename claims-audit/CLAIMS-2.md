## The specific hunts, in short

1. **Numbers that differ between surfaces.** There are three:
   - The Intel forward-add range is "1.06x to 1.20x" in the README, but "1.05–1.08×" plus 1.20× on the page; the lowest Intel job is 1.0545.
   - The Intel job count is "2 and 3 jobs", "5 jobs" on the page, and "one or two jobs each" in DETAILS; the data has 3 and 3 jobs with an aligned pair.
   - tiny-dnn's lost-loop line share is "0.030% of them" on the page and "0.07 %" in `realworld/README.md`: the page gives the share of NONE's instructions, the README the share of FAST's.

   All the other numbers that repeat across README, DETAILS, index and details agree.
2. **Real programs.**
   - Every instruction ratio, share, trap count and the 38/38, 12/12, 2/2 and 1/1 counts match the probe files and the census.
   - The estimate's arithmetic is right: share × (r − 1), with r = 8.014, the largest ratio in Figure 1. All 12 printed values match. It is labelled "An estimate, not a measurement", states its assumption, and nothing in it reads as a measured time.
   - "Not timed yet" holds: there is no time ratio.
   - The run annotation confirms that GitHub did not start the jobs. But the refused run, 38303777600, was the build run that the timing depends on, not "the timing run".
   - LightGBM's numbers come from a log. The run that wrote it was cancelled before upload, and two of its rows used twice today's row count; the page does not say either.
3. **The "Tuned?" answer and the rule history.**
   - All five hashes exist, and each change touches the rule file it is said to touch. Every stated order holds against the run records.
   - The 1.599× and 1.603× that the page says were "seen" before v2 come from the pooled runs, which started a day after v2. What was seen before v2 was S1: 1.619×, interval to 1.643×.
   - "Partly, twice" undercounts: v3 also came after results were seen.
4. **Intel.** The page counts Xeon 8370C in 2 jobs and Xeon 8573C in 3 (5 jobs). The data has 8370C in 3 jobs (the S4 smoke run's j1, at 1.0565, is missing) and 8573C in 3 jobs with an aligned pair (plus the S2 job, which has no aligned build). The range 1.05 to 1.08 still holds over all 6 jobs.
5. **Links to the original repository.**
   - None in `site/` (including `site/data/`), `README.md` or `readme-parts/`.
   - Still on public-facing files: `mitigate/README.md` (3 run links), `siblings/README.md` (2), and `docs/dayone-results.md` (1), which DETAILS links from its S0 row.
   - Also in the day-one JSON files, and 85 run records under `results/` (`workflow_run_url`).
6. **Google.**
   - No surface (page text with every fold open, README, DETAILS, folder READMEs, learn) says that 0.30% holds or fails, or says anything about Google's services.
   - The S1 to S3 run records under `results/`, and `results/fixture.json`, still carry the retired harness line "Google's 0.30% holds for forward loops ...". The S4 records and the site data are clean.
7. **Strong words.**
   - "recover the cost" in the page's meta description.
   - "near 1× on the AMD models" and "about nothing" for clang (the AVX2 forward add is 1.11× on EPYC 9V45).
   - "largest single factor" in the NOVEC footnote.
   - "Most of the extra instructions ... from checks elsewhere".
   - "every", "stable", "safe", "rare" and "cheap" are used within the data.
8. **`tools/lostloops/README.md`.** Its first line says plainly that the live run with real clang has not been tested. The test section says the tests use saved records and a fake compiler. No lostloops workflow run exists. Its numbers match the mitigation data.
9. **The 8 kernels and the pre-registration.** `kernels/` has 8 `.cpp` files. `corpus/projects2.json` and `corpus/PLAN2.md` were first added in 2e67a6b (14:30:30Z). Every corpus run before that commit has a head that does not contain it. The first run that could compile census 2, 38264388761, started at 14:38:26Z. The order holds.
